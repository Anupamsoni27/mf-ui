20 Ways to Analyze Stock Price vs Fund Holdings

Review these 20 distinct interactive charts utilizing advanced visual architectures, moving averages, and aggregations.

1. Classic Combo (Bar & Line) - The standard layout. Mutual fund actions are grounded as volume bars, stock trends flow over top.
2. Percentage Growth Overlay - Shows the actual % return and % holding change from Day 1. Perfect for seeing correlation directly on a shared Y-axis.
11. Role Reversal (Price Bars + Fund Line) - Flips the standard logic. Massive background bars define the price range level, while a distinct sharp line tracks mutual fund decisions.
12. Daily Price Action (Waterfall) - Transforms price action into blocks, revealing the magnitude of daily gaps and sequences of continuous positive/negative momentum.
13. Price Concentration (Histogram) - Bins the stock price into ranges to expose horizontal "support" and "resistance" zones where the stock has spent the most days.
14. Up vs Down Days (Donut) - A high-level proportion analysis aggregating exactly how many days the stock trended upward vs downward over this period.
15. Moving Average Envelope (Trend Area) - Automatically plots a 5-day smooth average underlying the volatile daily price line to establish the core trajectory.
16. Price Momentum Oscillator (MACD Style) - Instead of raw price, this visualizes pure "Velocity" by plotting the difference between the current price and its 7-day trailing average.
17. Buy/Sell Signal Scatter - Colors raw timeline scatter plot points dynamically: Green dots mean funds added that day, Red means funds exited!
18. Cumulative Gain Tracking - Plots the raw numerical total of points the stock has rallied continuously from its lowest baseline over the period.
19. Pure Holding Volatility - Completely hides the stock price. This focuses 100% on plotting the explicit minute changes (+/- 5 funds) in standard institutional backing.
20. Triple Metric Overlay (Volume Sync) - The ultimate master view. Plots Price Line, Smooth Trend Line, and Holding Count Bars simultaneously in a unified high-density chart.
3. Dual Smooth Lines (Spline) - Removes sharp edges for both metrics to easily identify rolling macro trends and momentum shifts.
4. Area & Line Hybrid - Shades the entire holdings area. Highlights the sheer "mass" of institutional holding beneath the stock price line.
5. Price & Volume Bubble Plot - Timeline dots track the price, but the SIZE of the dots reflects the number of funds holding the stock.
6. Correlation Matrix (No Timeline) - Plots Price vs Holdings directly. If dots trend Up-Right, funds buy as price goes up. If Up-Left, funds sell as price goes up.
7. Step-Line Trajectory - Treats data as discrete "steps". Instantly exposes exact days where sharp selling/buying cascades happen.
8. Dual Area Highlights - Semi-transparent overlapping areas instantly highlight divergence (where areas cross over or separate wildly).
9. Target Markers (Scatter + Bar) - Precise price targets shown as crosshairs over the holding bars instead of a confusing continuous line.
10. Pure Dual Scatter - No connects, just pure raw data points. Avoids tricking the eye into seeing momentum that isn't really there.
