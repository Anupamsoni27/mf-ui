# Stockdash User Guide

## 🎯 Platform Overview

**Stockdash** is a financial intelligence platform designed to help you track **institutional money flow** in the Indian stock market. It answers a critical question: _"Which mutual funds are buying or selling this stock?"_

By tracking mutual fund holdings, you can gauge institutional confidence in a company, often considered "smart money."

### Key Benefits

- **Identify Institutional Trends**: See if mutual funds are accumulating (buying) or distributing (selling) a stock over time.
- **Validate Investment Ideas**: Check if top-performing funds hold the stocks you are interested in.
- **Discover Opportunities**: Find stocks with high mutual fund interest that you might have missed.

---

## 🚀 How to Use the App

### 1. Browse & Search Stocks

- Navigate to the **Stocks** page.
- Use the **Search Bar** to find specific companies (e.g., "Reliance", "TCS").
- **Pro Tip**: Sort the list by **"Holding Count"**. This ranks stocks by how many mutual funds hold them, instantly showing you the most popular stocks among institutions.

### 2. Analyze Stock Details

Click on any stock to view its detailed dashboard. Here is what to look for:

- **Fund Count**: The total number of mutual funds currently holding this stock.
  - _High number_ = High institutional confidence.
  - _Low number_ = Low institutional interest.
- **Holdings Timeline (Chart)**: Look at the trend line over the last few quarters.
  - **↗ Upward Trend**: Funds are _buying_. Positive signal.
  - **↘ Downward Trend**: Funds are _selling_. Negative/Cautionary signal.
  - **→ Flat**: Stable holdings.

### 3. Deep Dive into Fund Holdings

On the stock details page, look at the **Fund Holdings** list.

- This shows _exactly which_ mutual funds own the stock.
- Look for names of reputable funds (e.g., SBI Bluechip, HDFC Top 100). If quality funds are holding it, it adds credibility.

### 4. Create Your Watchlist (Login Required)

- Click **Login** to create a personal account.
- Use the **Favorites** (Heart icon) feature to save interesting stocks.
- View your saved stocks on your personal **Dashboard** to track them easily without searching every time.

## 🎓 Making Investment Decisions

| Signal                 | What it Means                       | Action Consideration                                              |
| :--------------------- | :---------------------------------- | :---------------------------------------------------------------- |
| **Rising Fund Count**  | More funds are entering the stock.  | **Potential Buy**: Institutional demand is increasing.            |
| **Falling Fund Count** | Funds are exiting the stock.        | **Potential Sell/Avoid**: Smart money might be losing confidence. |
| **High Stability**     | Fund count remains high and steady. | **Hold**: Strong long-term conviction from institutions.          |

> **⚠️ Disclaimer**: This tool analyzes _historical_ holdings. Always combine this data with your own fundamental and technical research.
